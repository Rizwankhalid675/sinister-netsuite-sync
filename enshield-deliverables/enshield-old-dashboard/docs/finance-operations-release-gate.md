# Finance operations release gate

This phase is a shadow operational ledger only. It permits drafts, internal approvals,
reports, and recording a payment confirmation that was independently executed and
verified outside Enshield.

Until written owner/CPA approval, there is:

- no payment initiation;
- no automatic tax calculation or filing;
- no FX conversion or remeasurement;
- no bank feed;
- no external accounting sync or posting.

Production activation also requires approved legal entities, chart of accounts,
recognition policy, reserve policy, payment authority, retention, currencies, fiscal
calendar, and migration/reconciliation sign-off.
